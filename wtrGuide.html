Want to Read is now TBR

Since version 17.0, Want to Read is part of Lists.

Want to Read became TBR (To Be Read), a special list inside the new Lists. Your queue, its rules and your AO3 bookmark tag are all still there, and you can now also create your own lists.

💡
Where to go now

Everything about TBR and lists is in the Lists & TBR Guide. For TBR itself, go straight to TBR: To Be Read.
